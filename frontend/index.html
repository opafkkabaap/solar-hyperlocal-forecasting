Project Summary & Mission

La Trobe University · UniSolar

The Helios Solar Intelligence System provides high-accuracy weather forecasting, physical PV power prediction, and dispatch optimization for solar installations across Victoria, Australia.

Physics-Informed Architecture

Fuses astronomical geometry (zenith, elevation, azimuth) and Ineichen clear-sky radiation curves with XGBoost and PyTorch Deep Bi-GRU sequences.

🌐
Multi-Campus Synchronization

Harmonizes 5 Victorian campuses (Bundoora, Wodonga, Bendigo, Mildura, Shepparton), converting UTC satellite irradiance to local Eastern Time with exact solar noon alignment.

⚡
Actionable Energy Optimization

Determines optimal panel tilt, attributes thermodynamic losses (cell heating, cloud cover, inverter clipping), and schedules battery storage to minimize peak grid draw.

Clear-Sky Index Normalization
k_t = GHI / GHI_clearsky

Normalizes solar radiation against the cloudless physical envelope, removing diurnal cycles from the regression model.

Faiman PV Cell Temperature
T_cell = T_air + GHI / (u_0 + u_1 · W_s)

Models module temperature rise as a function of ambient temperature, irradiance, and wind speed cooling.

Temperature-Derated DC Power
P_ideal = kWp · (GHI/1000) · [1 - 0.0038(T_cell - 25)]

Quantifies silicon crystalline temperature coefficient derating (-0.38%/°C) above standard test condition.

Forecast Skill Score
SS = (1 - RMSE_model / RMSE_persistence) × 100%

Evaluates percentage accuracy advantage over standard 24-hour diurnal seasonal persistence.

Predictive Power Flow & State-of-Charge Schedule

How Predictive Dispatch Works:

  1. Pass 1 (Solar Capture): During daytime hours (10:00 – 15:00), generation exceeding building load directly charges the battery up to its 95% SoC buffer.
  2. Pass 2 (Binary Search Peak Shaving): Using the 24-hour forecasted generation curve, the optimizer calculates the exact flat threshold power to flatten the evening demand peak (17:00 – 21:00), completely eliminating grid demand spikes.
